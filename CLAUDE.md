# CLAUDE.md — Aurvex Website Build Rules

## IDENTITY
You are building websites for local service businesses in the greater LA area. Every site must look like it was built by a premium agency, not generated by AI. No generic layouts. No stock-photo feel. No corporate filler text.

## DESIGN RULES — NEVER BREAK
- NO generic hero sections with "Welcome to Our Company" headlines
- NO blue/white color schemes unless client specifically requests
- NO Lorem ipsum or placeholder text ever
- NO centered text walls
- NO clipart or emoji icons in navigation
- NO default system fonts (Arial, Times New Roman)
- ALWAYS mobile first — test every section at 375px width
- ALWAYS use real business name, real phone number, real services
- ALWAYS include a visible call to action above the fold
- ALWAYS use Google Fonts — preferred: Inter, Sora, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans

## COLOR SYSTEM
Pick ONE of these palettes per project — never mix:
- DARK PROFESSIONAL: #0A0A0A background, #FFFFFF text, #C9A84C gold accent
- SLATE MODERN: #1E293B background, #F8FAFC text, #38BDF8 blue accent
- WARM TRADE: #1A1208 background, #F5F0E8 text, #D97706 amber accent
- CLEAN WHITE: #FFFFFF background, #111827 text, #2563EB blue accent

## TYPOGRAPHY RULES
- Headlines: Bold, 48px+ on desktop, 32px+ on mobile
- Body: 16px minimum, 1.6 line height
- Never use more than 2 font families per site
- Font pairings: Sora (headings) + Inter (body) | Outfit (headings) + Inter (body)

## SECTION ORDER FOR SERVICE BUSINESS SITES
1. Nav — logo left, phone number right, CTA button
2. Hero — headline with problem/solution, subtext, two CTAs, real image or dark overlay
3. Services — 3-4 cards with icons, short descriptions
4. Why Us — 3 differentiators, specific not generic
5. Reviews — real testimonials with names and star ratings
6. Contact/CTA — phone, email, address, contact form
7. Footer — copyright, email, phone

## COPY RULES
- Headlines must name the specific trade: "Cerritos' Most Trusted Roofing Crew" not "Welcome to Our Company"
- Every section must answer: what do you do, who is it for, why should I trust you
- Use real numbers when available: "200+ jobs completed" "15 years serving Cerritos"
- CTA buttons say: "Call Now", "Get a Free Quote", "Book Today" — never "Submit" or "Click Here"

## WHAT TO ASK CLIENT BEFORE BUILDING
1. Exact company name
2. Services offered (be specific)
3. Phone number and email
4. Physical address or service area
5. Photos of their work (real photos always beat stock)
6. Any existing reviews or testimonials
7. Domain name if they have one
8. Preferred colors or reference sites they like

## CONSTRUCTION COMPANY SPECIFIC
- Show before/after project photos prominently
- License number if available builds massive trust
- Emergency availability if applicable
- Service area map or list of cities served
- Free estimate CTA is the strongest hook

## QUALITY CHECK BEFORE DELIVERING
- [ ] Opens perfectly on iPhone (375px)
- [ ] Phone number is clickable on mobile
- [ ] No placeholder text anywhere
- [ ] Page loads in under 3 seconds
- [ ] All links work
- [ ] Contact form submits correctly
- [ ] Color contrast passes readability test
- [ ] Business name appears in browser tab title
